## Notes

- The five `*_signature.png` / `*_Signature.png` files are the social-proof toast
  thumbnails in `CesProofWidget`.
- The `Screenshot-2025-09-08-*` and `Dashboard`/`Editor`/`Design` files are product
  screenshots of the original app, used in the Our Platform carousel and the
  How-it-Works cards. They also show the original app's UI, not yours.
- `Content-Container*.avif` and `Card-Graphic-Container*` are the composed
  illustrations inside the Numbers and Features cards.

Regenerate this list any time with:

```bash
swiftc -O /tmp/ocr.swift -o /tmp/ocr   # Vision-based OCR helper
find public/ces/img -type f \( -name '*.png' -o -name '*.jpg' -o -name '*.webp' -o -name '*.avif' \) \
  -print0 | xargs -0 -n 40 /tmp/ocr > /tmp/ocr-out.tsv
```
